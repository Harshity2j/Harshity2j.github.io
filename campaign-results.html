How to read these figures

The supplied snapshots do not include full date ranges, attribution windows or account exports. The fractional Canadian purchase figure is reproduced as a platform-reported metric, not rounded into a count of people. The exact explanation for the fractional value cannot be confirmed from the snapshot alone.

The two examples should not be treated as a like-for-like comparison. Revenue, purchase value and attributed conversion numbers can have different definitions. A complete review needs spend, dates, attribution settings, tracking quality and booking-system records.

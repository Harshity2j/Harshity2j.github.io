Google Ads for Booking Businesses: A Practical Starting Plan

By Harshit Pandey · Published 10 October 2026

A booking business needs more than clicks. It needs the right people to understand the experience, find an available time and complete a booking. For an escape room or another location-based experience, start by connecting those steps before increasing advertising spend.

1. Define a booking you can measure

Write down the action that matters: a paid online booking, a confirmed enquiry or a phone call that becomes a booking. These are different outcomes. A click on a booking button does not prove that a customer completed a purchase. If the booking journey moves to another domain, review that handoff and confirm that completed bookings can be measured.

Keep one primary outcome clear. Secondary actions, such as viewing a room or opening a contact page, can help diagnose the journey without being reported as completed bookings.

2. Match the search to the experience

Organise campaigns around the services and locations customers can actually book. Review searches for city names, nearby experiences and the type of activity you offer. A search for a team-building experience may need a different landing page from a search for a weekend activity with friends.

Read the search terms report to identify irrelevant intent. Examples may include job searches, free online games or unrelated products. Check each term against your offering before excluding it; a word that is irrelevant to one business may be useful to another.

3. Give the visitor a clear next step

A useful landing page explains the experience, the location, group size, available times and how to book. Show the information customers need before asking them to act. On mobile, check that the booking button is visible, readable and easy to use.

Test the entire journey yourself. An attractive page cannot compensate for unavailable slots, a confusing booking process or a broken payment step.

4. Review business outcomes alongside campaign data

Look at attributed bookings, cost per booking and reported revenue together. Compare those figures with actual bookings and the capacity you can sell. Attribution windows and platform definitions can differ, so a platform total should not be presented as a perfect record of every sale.

A supplied US escape-room portfolio snapshot reports $102,140 revenue, 357 bookings, 3.32× ROAS and $81.22 CPA. These are historical campaign figures with their own context, not targets promised for a new business. Read the portfolio results and limitations.

What should you prepare before starting?

Share your service area, booking website, current advertising setup, existing conversion data and business goals. These inputs help identify the biggest gaps and agree a realistic scope. Start with a measured plan, then adjust based on outcomes rather than assuming a budget increase will solve every problem.

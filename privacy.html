Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy describes how information is handled when you use Harshit Pandey’s portfolio, its enquiry forms and its portfolio assistant.

Information you choose to provide

The contact forms collect your name, email address, selected service and message. The assistant’s enquiry form also asks for your business or website. These details are used to respond to your enquiry, discuss your requirements and coordinate a potential project. Do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

The portfolio assistant

The assistant answers using predefined information from this website. It is not connected to a general-purpose AI model. Questions are processed in your browser; the current implementation does not send the conversation to Harshit or an AI provider, or save it in browser storage. A page reload clears that conversation. The separate enquiry form sends only the fields you submit.

Enquiry processing and email

Enquiry forms are submitted to FormSubmit and addressed to harshity5j@gmail.com. You may be asked to complete verification before submission. FormSubmit processes the form data as a third-party service; consult its information at formsubmit.co. Email providers also process enquiries when messages are delivered or answered.

Hosting, technical information and cookies

The hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs to deliver and protect the site. They may use security cookies. External Google Fonts requests may also send technical information to the font provider. The current website code does not install Google Analytics or advertising pixels. Third-party service pages have their own data practices.

Sharing and retention

Enquiry details are used to handle your request and may be processed by the form, hosting and email services needed to operate the site. They are not collected for a public directory. Enquiry records may remain in the receiving email account while needed for correspondence, project records or applicable obligations. This site does not promise a fixed retention period for third-party providers.

Your choices

You can browse without submitting an enquiry. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information you sent to Harshit, email harshity5j@gmail.com. Requests will be considered subject to identity checks, any ongoing project needs and applicable obligations. Provider-held records may require a separate request to that provider.

External links and updates

Links may take you to other websites whose policies apply there. This policy will be updated if the website’s data handling changes. The effective date above identifies this version.

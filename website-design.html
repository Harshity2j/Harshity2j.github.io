What we plan before design

We agree the pages, content, target customers, primary action and required integrations. An ecommerce build may need catalogue, payment and fulfilment requirements; a service website may need lead routing and qualification. Those decisions set the scope.

Mobile and SEO foundations

The site should have readable text, usable buttons, stable layouts and accessible navigation. Search foundations include descriptive titles, useful content, appropriate canonical links, internal links and a sitemap. An animated experience can sit alongside a readable version, as it does on my portfolio.

Content before decoration

Visitors should find your service, offer and proof quickly. Design helps communicate those things. It should not make someone complete a long tour to find a phone number or understand your business.
